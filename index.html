Bio

Short Bio: Born in 1994, Ramia hails from the valleys of Wakefield, Trelawny, Jamaica. Ramia is a compelling voice in contemporary reggae music with piercing lyricism born from deep contemplation. Ramia's soundscape merges the hypnotic basslines of traditional dub music with the soulful soaring melodies of roots reggae. Ramia's music provides both an auditory sanctuary and rest for wounded souls.

Extended Bio: Ramia’s musical DNA is biological, with his father being a Rastafarian and musician who introduced him to reggae music in his formative years. The second column of his musical foundation was forged at church through the singing of hymns and other sacred songs. Growing up, the week began with the pristine, uplifting harmonies of traditional church hymns and unfolded to the steady, warm crackle of classic reggae records spinning at home. This dual upbringing instilled a deep respect for melody and message.

For Ramia, music is never merely entertainment; it is a sacred mirror. It is an intentional tool designed to reflect the realities of the world and inspire listeners to face themselves. Ramia’s lyricism effortlessly navigates the pillars of love, social commentary and spiritual searching.

Through captivating storytelling and honest reflection, Ramia uses the mic as a weapon for change and a balm for the soul. In a world hungry for substance, Ramia delivers a sound that honors the foundations of reggae while pushing the genre forward with urgent, heartfelt relevance.
